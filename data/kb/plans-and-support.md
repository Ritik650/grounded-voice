# Meridian Broadband — Plans and Support

*Fictional company, invented for this demo.*

## Residential plans

The Starter plan provides 100 megabits per second download and 20 megabits upload for
thirty dollars a month. It includes one router and a 500 gigabyte monthly data
allowance.

The Standard plan provides 500 megabits per second download and 100 megabits upload for
fifty dollars a month. It includes one router, unlimited data, and a static IP address
on request.

The Gigabit plan provides 1000 megabits per second download and 500 megabits upload for
eighty dollars a month. It includes a Meridian Mesh router, unlimited data, and a static
IP address.

## Contract terms

All residential plans are month to month. There is no minimum contract and no early
termination fee. Plan changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle, except
upgrades, which take effect immediately and are prorated.

## Installation

Standard installation is free and takes about two hours. The earliest appointment is
usually three business days out. Installation in buildings that need new cabling is
quoted separately and typically costs one hundred and twenty dollars.

## Support hours

Phone and chat support run from 7 a.m. to 11 p.m. local time, seven days a week.
Emergency outage support runs 24 hours a day.

The support line is 1-800-555-0142. This number is fictional.

## Outages

Planned maintenance is announced at least 48 hours in advance by email and SMS, and
takes place between 2 a.m. and 5 a.m.

If your service is down for more than four continuous hours in a billing cycle, you are
eligible for a service credit of one day of service per four hours of downtime. Credits
are applied automatically and appear on the next invoice.
