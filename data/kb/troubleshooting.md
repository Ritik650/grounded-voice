# Meridian Broadband — Troubleshooting

*Fictional company, invented for this demo.*

## No connection at all

First, check whether the router's power light is solid white. If it is off, the router
has no power. If it is blinking amber, the router has power but no signal from the line.

If the power light is blinking amber, restart the router by unplugging it for thirty
seconds. A restart takes about three minutes to complete. If it is still amber after a
restart, the line itself is likely down and you should report a fault.

## Slow speeds

Run a speed test from a device connected by ethernet cable rather than over wifi. Wifi
speeds are typically 30 to 50 percent lower than the wired speed, which is normal.

If the wired speed is below half your plan speed, restart the router first. If the
problem persists after a restart, report a fault.

## Wifi drops on some devices

Devices that only support the 2.4 gigahertz band will drop when the router steers them
to 5 gigahertz. In the account portal, under Wifi Settings, you can split the two bands
into separate network names, which stops the steering.

## Reporting a fault

Faults can be reported in the account portal or by calling support. A technician
callback is offered within four hours during support hours.

If an engineer visit is required, the earliest slot is usually the next business day.
Engineer visits are free when the fault is on Meridian's side of the connection. If the
fault is inside your home and not caused by Meridian equipment, the visit costs sixty
dollars.

## Router replacement

Routers are covered for the life of the service. A faulty router is replaced free of
charge and arrives within two business days. Routers damaged by the customer are
replaced for forty dollars.
