# Meridian Broadband — Billing

*Meridian Broadband is a fictional company invented for this demo. Nothing here
describes a real provider, and none of these policies apply to anyone.*

## Billing cycle

Meridian Broadband bills monthly in advance. Your billing date is the day of the month
on which your service was activated. If your service was activated on the 29th, 30th or
31st, you are billed on the 28th in months that are shorter.

Invoices are issued as PDFs to the email address on the account and are also available
in the Meridian account portal for 24 months.

## Payment methods

We accept direct debit, credit card, and UPI. Direct debit is the only method eligible
for the two dollar monthly paperless discount.

Payment is collected three business days after the invoice is issued. If a payment
fails, we retry once after five business days.

## Late payments

Accounts more than 14 days overdue are charged a late fee of five dollars. Accounts
more than 45 days overdue are suspended. Suspended accounts are restored within two
hours of payment clearing.

We do not charge a reconnection fee for accounts restored within 90 days of suspension.

## Refunds

If you cancel mid-cycle, unused service is refunded pro rata to the original payment
method within ten business days.

Equipment returned in working condition within 30 days of cancellation is refunded in
full. After 30 days, no equipment refund is available.
